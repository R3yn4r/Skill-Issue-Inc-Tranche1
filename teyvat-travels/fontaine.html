THE CITY OF JUSTICE

A city of law, art and water

Fontaine is a city-state built around water, where elegant streets, aquabuses and grand courtrooms share the same shoreline. Its people take pride in order, fairness and a good performance.

From the courts above the city to the stage below, every corner of Fontaine is designed to be seen, judged and remembered.

Fontaine

01

Court of Fontaine

This city alone stands above all waters. Towering up at the highest point of the city, the Palais Mermonia represents the highest authority in Fontaine. Under its gaze, the people lead orderly lives that are as regular and predictable as the aquabus. Law serves to maintain a stable order, which is in turn the cornerstone that equity and justice are built upon.

Best for: Grand architecture

Fontaine

02

Opera Epiclese

A large opera house situated in Erinnyes that also serves as the symbol of trials and judgment. The true and the fantastical, the comedic and tragic find a stage here equally. Perhaps it truly is as an ancient Fontainian playwright once said: All the world's a stage, and all of us merely players.

Best for: Evening shows

Fontaine

03

Lucine Fountain

At the heart of the grand plaza before the opera house, the Lucine Fountain rises in tiers of pale stone and gold, sending bright jets of water into the pools below. Fontainians meet here between hearings and performances, and travelers come for the view of the opera house rising behind the spray. Few places show the city's devotion to water so completely.

Best for: Photos and relaxing

Before you go

Travel Tips

Where to stay

Stay near the central plaza so the courts, the opera house and the fountain are within walking distance.

What to see and do

Book an evening performance, then visit the fountain at dusk when the plaza is at its liveliest.

Getting around

The city's aquabuses connect the main districts, and the central streets are easy to walk.
