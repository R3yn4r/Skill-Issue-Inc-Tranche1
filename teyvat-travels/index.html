Who We Are

A team for the journey

Skill Issue Inc. is the team behind Teyvat Travels, a travel agency for explorers who want to travel across the seven nations of Teyvat. We are the researchers, writers, designers, and developers who turn a vast world into clear, well-planned journeys. Behind every destination guide, itinerary, and travel tip is a group of specialists who study each region in depth, map out the best routes, and keep every page accurate and up to date, so travelers can fully experience the joy of adventure with ease.

FAQ

A few helpful details

Is Teyvat Travels an official service?

No. Teyvat Travels is an unofficial fan project by Skill Issue Inc. and is not affiliated with or endorsed by HoYoverse.

Can I book a real-world trip through the site?

No. Teyvat is a fictional world, and this site is a fan-made guide. It does not sell travel or booking services.

Where should I begin exploring?

Start with the nation that interests you most in the Choose Your Nation section, then use its guide to explore local highlights and routes.

How can I contact Skill Issue Inc.?

Email the team at skillissueinc@gmail.com.
