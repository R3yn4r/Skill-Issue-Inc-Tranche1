The nation of contracts

A harbor kingdom rooted in stone

Liyue sits where the sea meets the mountains, where harbor trade and ancient myth have shaped one another for centuries. The bright bustle of Liyue Harbor moves with merchants, sailors, and travelers from every corner of Teyvat, while the peaks above it keep the stories of older kingdoms alive.

Under Morax, Liyue became known for its discipline, prosperity, and sense of order. Every cliff, terrace, and caravan route tells a story of labor, faith, and survival — a landscape as exacting as it is beautiful.

Points of interest

Key Locations

Liyue Harbor

The sprawling, prosperous merchant city that serves as the heart of the Liyue region. Governed by the Qixing, it is known as the "City of Contracts" where ships gather from all corners of Teyvat. It is famous for its bustling docks, lantern-lit streets, and the annual Rite of Descension.

Best for: Shopping & culture

Mt. Aocang

A serene, mist-shrouded peak located high in the Jueyun Karst. It serves as the domain of the adeptus Cloud Retainer. At its center lies a tranquil pool and a stone dining table famously set with three chairs, where the adepti occasionally gather far above the mortal world.

Best for: Mountain scenery

Wangshu Inn

A towering inn built directly into a massive, golden-leaved tree in the center of Dihua Marsh. While it appears to be just a popular resting spot for traveling merchants, it actually serves as a secret base of operations for the Qixing's agents and a quiet vantage point for the vigilant Yaksha, Xiao.

Best for: Views

Wangshu Inn

A landmark within Dihua Marsh, built upon a massive stone pillar. As most of the patrons that stop here are traveling merchants, the inn provides an area for them to trade and set up stalls. The view from the top of the inn is jaw-dropping — weather permitting you can see all the way to Mt. Qingce and Jueyun Karst in the distance. Legend amongst the stalls says that the tower of the inn hides a mysterious secret, but that's as far as the legend goes, as no-one has ever been able to validify the claims.

Best for: Views

Qingce Village

At the northernmost point of Liyue, hidden between the hills and the bamboo forests sits Qingce Village. White rolling clouds that sit in the valleys between the mountains are embellished with row upon row of crescent-moon water paddies. When the wind blows, the ripples in the water give off an array of colors.

Best for: Countryside

Jueyun Karst

Dizzying peaks that sit shrouded in mist and clouds in the northwest of Liyue. The area hides many stunning views, but owing to tracks found on the complex paths leading up the peaks, a legend has formed that the hidden home of the Adepti lies somewhere in the area. Though another factor may simply be the hopes of those that seek blessings from the Adepti. Given the dangers within, the area is not suitable for outsiders. Best viewed from afar at Wangshu Inn.

Best for: Scenery

Wangshu Inn

Wangshu Inn rises from a stone pillar in the middle of Dihua Marsh, giving it one of the most memorable views in Liyue. Merchants and travelers use it as a resting place, a marketplace, and a place to trade, while the tower itself becomes a landmark of stories and rumor. From the top, the view opens toward Mt. Qingce and Jueyun Karst, and the atmosphere carries the feeling that a hidden secret is waiting just beyond the horizon, even if no one can prove it.

Best for: Views

Liyue

05

Qingce Village

At the northern edge of Liyue, Qingce Village sits between hills and bamboo groves in one of the region's most peaceful valleys. Its fields are cut into crescent-shaped terraces that catch the wind and reflect changing light in ripples of color. The clouds that gather between the mountains seem to hover just above the paddies, giving the place a calm, dreamlike rhythm. It feels quiet in the best possible way — as if the landscape itself is taking a breath.

Best for: Countryside

Liyue

06

Jueyun Karst

Jueyun Karst is defined by sharp, cloud-wrapped peaks rising in the northwest of Liyue, where every trail seems to lead to another hidden view. The legends around the region speak of the Adepti and a secret home concealed somewhere among the misty slopes, while the mountain paths themselves keep the place dangerous and distant for outsiders. The drama of the terrain is difficult to capture in a single glance; from a safe distance, it becomes one of the most awe-filled landscapes in the nation.

Best for: Scenery

Before you go

Travel Tips

Where to stay

Base yourselves in Liyue Harbor for the easiest access to the markets, ferries, and long mountain routes. It is the most practical place to start and end each day.

What to eat & drink

Try warm buns, fresh seafood, and tea from the harbor stalls. Liyue meals are practical, comforting, and designed to keep travelers going through long days on the road.

Getting around

Use the harbor as your anchor, then branch out by foot, caravan, or guide for mountain destinations. Longer routes are best planned around daylight and weather.
