The city of freedom

An island city on Cider Lake

Mondstadt sits on an island in the middle of Cider Lake, surrounded by mountains and wide open plains. The city is known across Teyvat for its wines, its music, and the quiet pride its people take in living as they choose.

Gentle breezes carry the scent of dandelions across the lake — a gift, they say, from Barbatos, the Anemo God who watches over the city without ever demanding anything in return.

Points of interest

Key Locations

Cider Lake

A natural freshwater lake featuring a wide, crystalline appearance that has remained unchanged for eons — so clear you can see the bottom, and cool and refreshing to drink. It is none other than these headwaters that brought Mondstadt's ciders and wines to fame and made the lake the symbol of Mondstadt that it is today.

Best for: Scenery

Falcon Coast

Stretching eastward from Windrise to the sea, Falcon Coast — together with Cape Oath — shelters the sea from three sides, creating a bay that sits east of Mondstadt. Given the long coastline and shallow waters, citizens of Mondstadt often come here to enjoy a nice stroll. On occasion, you can see falcons soaring high above the blue waves that brush upon the white sand.

Best for: Relaxing walks

Cape Oath

In the southeast corner of Mondstadt's surrounds lies a cape that borders with Galesong Hill. Legend has it two lovers made an oath to each other here and left behind a deeply touching story. To this day the area is still known to be popular amongst young lovebirds. Other than being a great vantage point for watching sunrises and sunsets, the drop in terrain makes it easy to see Windrise, which sits facing the sea.

Best for: Sunsets

Dawn Winery

Nestled in the land southwest of Mondstadt sits the winery that has passed down the legacy of Mondstadt's traditional wines and ciders for generations. The grapes grown here are used to produce a range of wines that are popular right across Teyvat. Every year's products are split, with a portion being sold locally within Mondstadt, the rest is sold off via the trade route that sits south of the city.

Best for: Wine & cider

Springvale

A sleepy little village to the south of Mondstadt, that sits facing the lake that surrounds the city. Most of the village's residents are hunters that provide fresh meat for all the big restaurants of Mondstadt. Springvale's rustic appeal attracts many tourists. If you are lucky in your travels, you may come across a restaurant that truly knows how to bring out the best qualities of Springvale's meat.

Best for: Local food

Thousand Winds Temple

A collection of temple ruins that sits northeast of Mondstadt. According to local legend, millennia ago this area was a temple for worshipping the Anemo God. Though it has been left to waste for centuries, leaving the ruins you see today. If you look closely at some of the stone pillars, you can still faintly make out some elegant carvings. This long-forgotten symbol of commemoration is engraved with the maxim: "Wind brings the seeds of tales, time allows them to take root."

Best for: History

Cider Lake

Cider Lake is a freshwater lake that wraps all the way around the island city of Mondstadt. The water is so clear you can see straight to the bottom, and cool enough to drink straight from the source. For centuries, it has been these same headwaters that gave Mondstadt's ciders and wines their distinctive taste, turning the lake into the city's most recognisable landmark. Standing on the shore at golden hour, with the city's towers reflected in the still water, is one of those moments you simply don't forget.

Best for: Scenery

Mondstadt

02

Falcon Coast

Falcon Coast is a shallow bay that stretches east of Mondstadt, sheltered on three sides by Cape Oath and the gentle curve of Windrise. The water stays calm enough that locals often walk the length of the white sand shore in bare feet, watching the tide come in. On most days you'll spot falcons riding the thermals above the waves — the coast takes its name from them. It's the kind of place that's easy to spend an entire afternoon without noticing the time pass.

Best for: Relaxing walks

Mondstadt

03

Cape Oath

Cape Oath juts out from Mondstadt's southeastern corner, where Galesong Hill meets the sea. Local legend says two lovers once stood here and swore an oath to each other, and the story still draws couples who come to watch the sun rise and set over the water. The drop in elevation makes it easy to pick out Windrise in the distance, standing tall against the horizon. Visit at dawn if you can — the light comes in low and warm, and the whole cape turns gold for about twenty minutes.

Best for: Sunsets

Mondstadt

04

Dawn Winery

Dawn Winery sits in the quiet countryside southwest of Mondstadt, and has been making the city's traditional wines and ciders for generations. The estate's rows of vines stretch across gently sloping hills, and during harvest season the whole area smells of ripe fruit. A portion of each year's crop stays in Mondstadt, sold from the winery's own cellar, while the rest heads south along the trade route that connects to the rest of Teyvat. If you visit, ask about the barrel-aged dandelion wine — it's the one locals actually drink.

Best for: Wine & cider

Mondstadt

05

Springvale

Springvale is a small hunters' village south of Mondstadt, sitting on the lakeshore with an unobstructed view of the city across the water. Most of its residents are hunters who supply fresh game to Mondstadt's restaurants, and the village has built a quiet reputation for meat that city kitchens can't easily replicate. The wooden houses and worn stone paths give it a rustic, unhurried character that draws visitors looking for a break from the city. If you're lucky, you might find a local cook who knows exactly what to do with a slow fire and a good cut.

Best for: Local food

Mondstadt

06

Thousand Winds Temple

The Thousand Winds Temple is a scattered collection of ruins northeast of Mondstadt, all that remains of what was once a place of worship for the Anemo God. The temple has been abandoned for centuries, and the stones have slowly given way to moss and wind-worn carvings, but if you look closely at the pillars you can still make out faint inscriptions. The most complete reads: "Wind brings the seeds of tales, time allows them to take root." Standing among the ruins at dusk, with the wind moving through the broken archways, it's easy to believe that saying is still true.

Best for: History

Before you go

Travel Tips

Where to stay

The Inn of the Last Good Night sits right on the main square — rooms are modest but clean, and you can hear the wind chimes all night.

What to eat & drink

Try the local cider and Dandelion Wine from Dawn Winery. For food, head to Springvale for fresh-caught and slow-smoked meats you won't find in the city.

Getting around

The city itself is easy to walk. Cider Lake, Falcon Coast, and the city gates form a natural loop — give yourself a half-day and you can cover all three on foot.
