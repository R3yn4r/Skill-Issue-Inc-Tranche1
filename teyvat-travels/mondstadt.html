The city of freedom

An island city on Cider Lake

Mondstadt sits on an island in the middle of Cider Lake, surrounded by mountains and wide open plains. The city is known across Teyvat for its wines, its music, and the quiet pride its people take in living as they choose.

Gentle breezes carry the scent of dandelions across the lake — a gift, they say, from Barbatos, the Anemo God who watches over the city without ever demanding anything in return.

Points of interest

Key Locations

Cider Lake

A natural freshwater lake featuring a wide, crystalline appearance that has remained unchanged for eons — so clear you can see the bottom, and cool and refreshing to drink. It is none other than these headwaters that brought Mondstadt's ciders and wines to fame and made the lake the symbol of Mondstadt that it is today.

Best for: Scenery

Windrise

Windrise is a hill area in Galesong Hill, in Mondstadt. It's said to be the place where Vennessa ascended to Celestia. She became a god and now watches over Mondstadt as the Falcon of the West, one of the Four Winds. A huge oak tree grows here, and people believe it grew after she ascended.

Best for: Relaxing

Grand Cathedral of Mondstadt

The Grand Cathedral of Mondstadt, also called Favonius Cathedral, stands in Mondstadt City in Starfell Valley. It is run by the Church of Favonius and is used to worship Barbatos, the Anemo Archon. It's one of the city's best-known landmarks and a calm place to visit.

Best for: Sunsets & Sunrises

Cider Lake

Cider Lake is a freshwater lake that wraps all the way around the island city of Mondstadt. The water is so clear you can see straight to the bottom, and cool enough to drink straight from the source. For centuries, it has been these same headwaters that gave Mondstadt's ciders and wines their distinctive taste, turning the lake into the city's most recognisable landmark. Standing on the shore at golden hour, with the city's towers reflected in the still water, is one of those moments you simply don't forget.

Best for: Scenery

Before you go

Travel Tips

Where to stay

The Inn of the Last Good Night sits right on the main square — rooms are modest but clean, and you can hear the wind chimes all night.

What to eat & drink

Try the local cider and Dandelion Wine from Dawn Winery. For food, head to Springvale for fresh-caught and slow-smoked meats you won't find in the city.

Getting around

The city itself is easy to walk. Cider Lake, Windrise, and the city gates form a natural loop — give yourself a half-day and you can cover all three on foot.
