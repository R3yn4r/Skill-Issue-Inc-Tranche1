The land of ash and valor

A nation shaped by fire

Natlan is a land of dramatic volcanic terrain, where ash-covered ridges meet glowing craters. Its communities gather often for celebrations, dances, and contests of strength.

The nation is also famous for its thermal springs, whose warm waters are said to restore both body and spirit after a long journey through the highlands.

Points of interest

Key Locations

Tepecac Stadium

A great open arena where the people of Natlan gather for contests, performances, and festivals. The stadium is busiest during celebrations, when the crowd fills the stone seats and the sound carries across the valley.

Best for: Festivals

Children of the Echoes Canyon

A deep canyon of red stone whose walls seem to call back every voice that passes through. Travelers come to hike the winding paths and listen to the echoes rolling between the cliffs.

Best for: Hiking

People of the Springs Basin

A basin of warm, mineral-rich pools surrounded by volcanic rock. Locals have bathed here for generations, and visitors often stay for hours soaking in the steam as the sun goes down.

Best for: Relaxation

Tepecac Stadium

Tepecac Stadium is the great open arena where Natlan's people come together for contests, performances, and celebrations. On festival days the stone seats fill with spectators, and cheers echo across the valley. Arrive early to find a seat with a view of the field, and stay for the evening performances when the torches are lit.

Best for: Festivals

Natlan

02

Children of the Echoes Canyon

The walls of this deep red canyon seem to answer back, sending every shout and song rolling between the cliffs. The winding paths are best taken slowly, with frequent pauses to listen. Many visitors come for the echoes alone, and leave with stories about what they heard.

Best for: Hiking

Natlan

03

People of the Springs Basin

Warm, mineral-rich pools sit in a basin ringed by dark volcanic rock, with steam rising into the air. Locals have bathed here for generations, and visitors often stay for hours. As the sun sets, the pools glow and the steam turns gold, which makes it a favorite time to soak.

Best for: Relaxation

Before you go

Travel Tips

Where to stay

Stay near the springs basin, where guesthouses offer warm pools and quiet rooms after a day of hiking.

What to eat & drink

Try the grilled meats and spiced dishes served at festival grounds. Drink plenty of water, since the heat and altitude can be tiring.

Getting around

Plan for steep paths and rough ground between sites. Good walking shoes and an early start will make the day far more comfortable.
