About Teyvat Travels

Your Gateway to the Seven Nations

Our Mission

To connect adventurers with the wonders of Teyvat through expertly curated, culturally immersive expeditions that honor each nation's heritage and transform every journey into a lifelong memory.

Our Vision

A world where every traveler experiences the full majesty of all seven nations — their landscapes, cultures, cuisines, and stories — with the care and expertise of those who have walked every path.

Our Story

Founded by a seasoned explorer who traversed all seven nations over a decade of solo adventures, Teyvat Travels was born from the belief that these extraordinary lands deserve to be experienced firsthand. What began as handwritten guidebooks shared with fellow travelers has grown into Teyvat's most trusted expedition company, with certified guides in every region and partnerships with local communities across all nations.

Information Desk

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a visa to travel between nations?

Teyvat Travels handles all inter-nation travel permits as part of your package. We process documentation for all seven nations, ensuring seamless border crossings. Travelers need only provide a valid Adventurer's Guild ID.

What is the best time to visit each nation?

Each nation has its own ideal season — our specialists can help you plan around festivals, weather, and regional highlights when you reach out through the form.

Can I customize my tour package?

Yes. Every expedition can be tailored to your pace, interests, and group size. Mention your preferences in your inquiry and our team will build a custom itinerary.

What should I pack for multi-nation expeditions?

Packing needs vary widely by region — from Mondstadt's mild breezes to Snezhnaya's frost. We send a detailed packing guide once your itinerary is confirmed.

Are tours suitable for families with children?

Many of our packages are family-friendly. Let us know your children's ages when booking so we can recommend the best fit.
