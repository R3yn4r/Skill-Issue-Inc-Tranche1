The land beneath the snow

A kingdom of ice and light

Snezhnaya lies in the far north of Teyvat, where snow covers the land for most of the year. Its grand palaces and frosted towers rise above valleys wrapped in ice, and the sky often glows with the aurora.

Despite the cold, the nation is known for its architecture, its winter festivals, and the warm alpine chalets that keep travelers comfortable under the stars.

Points of interest

Key Locations

Zapolyarny Palace Outskirts

The grounds surrounding the great palace, where frosted towers and carved walls stand against the sky. The outskirts are quieter than the palace itself, and they offer some of the best views of its architecture under the aurora.

Best for: Architecture

Frost-bound Valleys

Wide valleys locked in ice, with pine forests and frozen rivers winding between the hills. The stillness here makes it one of the most peaceful stops in the nation.

Best for: Nature walks

Glacial Outposts

Small settlements built against the glaciers, with cozy chalets and warm lanterns. They are a good place to rest, share a hot meal, and watch the aurora come out over the ice.

Best for: Aurora viewing

Zapolyarny Palace Outskirts

The grounds beyond the palace gates hold some of Snezhnaya's most striking architecture, with frosted towers and carved walls rising against the sky. The crowds thin out here, leaving room to take in the building's scale. When the aurora moves overhead, the palace glows in a way that is hard to forget.

Best for: Architecture

Frost-bound Valleys

Wide valleys lie locked under ice, with pine forests lining frozen rivers that wind between the hills. Few sounds carry here except the wind and the crunch of snow underfoot. It is one of the quietest places in the nation, and a good spot to slow down and walk without a plan.

Best for: Nature walks

Glacial Outposts

Small settlements built against the glaciers, with wooden chalets and lanterns glowing in the cold. Travelers gather here for a hot meal and a chance to get warm. When night falls, the outposts make the best place to watch the aurora roll across the ice.

Best for: Aurora viewing

Before you go

Travel Tips

Where to stay

Book a room in one of the alpine chalets near the glacial outposts. They are warm, quiet, and close to the best aurora viewing spots.

What to eat & drink

Warm stews and hot drinks are the local favorites. Pack layers and keep a thermos handy, since the cold is strong even indoors near the outposts.

Getting around

Plan on a full day of travel between stops, since the roads are slow in snow. Bring warm boots, gloves, and a headlamp for early evening walks.
