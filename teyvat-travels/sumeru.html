THE CITY OF WISDOM

Where scholars meet the rainforest

Sumeru is a nation of two worlds: dense, mystical rainforest canopies on one side and the vast desert on the other, linked by a long tradition of scholarship.

From the lecture halls of its great academy to the caravan routes at its frontier, every stop in Sumeru is an invitation to learn something new.

Sumeru City Academy

Rising from the roots of a great tree, the Sumeru City Academy is the nation's center of learning, where scholars gather to study, debate and share discoveries. Its pale spires and blue-glass roofs blend into the forest around it, and its wide stairway welcomes students and visitors alike. For travelers, it is the best place to feel how deeply Sumeru values knowledge.

Best for: Learning and architecture

Gandharva Ville

The city above the forest. "The city of wisdom is ours, as is the forest of ignorance. Atop the tree lies the remnants of the kingdom of lost dreams."

Best for: Forest views and photos

Caravan Ribat

The frontier beneath the high wall. The mighty wall defends against the searing sandstorms but has also blocked the spread of wisdom's blessing. Is divine wisdom not meant to shine on all people?

Best for: Frontier views

Before you go

Travel Tips

Where to stay

Stay in or near Sumeru City so the Academy is within easy reach.

What to see and do

Visit the Academy in the morning, then head up to Gandharva Ville for the forest views.

Getting around

Wear comfortable shoes for forest paths, and bring water if you plan to travel toward the desert frontier.
